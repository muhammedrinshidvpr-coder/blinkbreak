# Install BlinkBreak (about 1 minute)

BlinkBreak is a small, free Windows app that reminds you to **blink**, **look far away**,
**sit tall**, and **move** while you study or work on your laptop. It's private: no internet,
no account, and nothing leaves your computer.

**You need:** Windows 10 or 11. No admin password needed.

## 1. Download

Open the latest release:
**https://github.com/muhammedrinshidvpr-coder/blinkbreak/releases/latest**

Under **Assets**, click **`BlinkBreak_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe`** (about 2 MB).

## 2. Install

Open the downloaded file.

If a blue box says **"Windows protected your PC"**:

1. Click **More info**
2. Click **Run anyway**

This happens because the app is new and not paid-code-signed yet, not because anything is wrong.
The whole source code is public on GitHub for anyone to check.

Then follow the installer. It only takes a few seconds.

## 3. Use it

BlinkBreak starts in the **system tray**: the small icons at the bottom-right of your screen.
If you can't see it, click the **^** arrow there.

**Right-click the icon** for:

- **Open dashboard**: see today's breaks and change settings
- **Take a break now**
- **Pause 15 min**: handy for exams or online classes
- **Quit**

Reminders appear by themselves, stay a few seconds, and close on their own. They never
interrupt your typing, and they wait if a fullscreen video, game, or presentation is open.

## Handy settings

Open the dashboard → **Settings**:

- **Appearance**: Light, Dark, or follow Windows
- **Reminders**: turn each one on/off and choose how often
- **Quiet hours**: no reminders at night
- **Start with Windows**: on by default, so you never have to remember to open it

## Uninstall

Quit BlinkBreak from the tray, then go to Windows **Settings → Apps → Installed apps →
BlinkBreak → Uninstall**.
