# Code of Conduct

BlinkBreak is a small, friendly project about taking care of yourself. Please
bring the same care to how you treat each other.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and first-time contributors.
- Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, or unwelcome personal attention.
- Respect people's time: keep issues and PRs focused and on-topic.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and PRs that break these
standards, and may block people who repeatedly do so.

Report problems privately to **muhammedrinshidvpr@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
